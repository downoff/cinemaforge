## How to record

- Quiet room, soft furnishings. Avoid kitchens and bathrooms (echo).
- Phone is fine. Hold it about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly off to
  the side so plosives ("p", "b") do not thump.
- Record **one take per numbered block**, not one long take. Easier to redo.
- Leave 2 seconds of silence at the start and end of every take. I need that
  room tone to match levels.
- If you fumble a line, pause, then say it again. Do not restart the file.
  I will cut the good one.
- Voice memo / any recorder. Send whatever it produces: m4a, wav, mp3, all fine.
- Read it like you are explaining it to one engineer you respect. Not a pitch.
  Slightly slower than feels natural. Trust the pauses.

Send me the files and I will re-time the whole cut to your delivery.

*Questions here are statements. Do not lift at the end.*
